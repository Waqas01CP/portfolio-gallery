# A showcase of users' own sites

**Deferred 2026-10-12. Closed 2026-10-20:** reopened by the owner's
direction of 2026-10-18 and built as ADR-0016 (merged 2026-10-19,
pull request 10); every question in "What revisiting would have to
produce" is answered there. Kept as the record of why it waited.

## What was proposed

A page or a list, beside the gallery of designs, showing real people's
sites built with this template, each a link to a live site.

## Why it was not built

The brief of 2026-10-11 put it out of scope. It is a different kind of
contribution: a design is checked before merge and changes nothing a user
says; a listing is a link to someone else's live site, which can change
after review, and which carries a real person's name into this repository
(scope floor line 1 is about the owner's content, but a showcase brings
other people's). The gallery itself already shows what the template
produces, on sample content.

## What could and could not be compared

No users exist yet, so there is no demand to measure. The operator's site's
research 0005 found that the developer-portfolios list removes dead and
parked links weekly with an automated check: a listing needs upkeep that a
design does not.

## The trigger

Revisit when users open issues asking to be listed, or when the gallery
holds designs from several contributors. The first is the real trigger.

## What revisiting would have to produce

Evidence of demand; a rule for what a listed site must meet (live, no
tracking, the credit kept?); and an answer to how a dead or changed link is
found and removed without the owner checking each by hand.
