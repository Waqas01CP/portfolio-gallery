# More than one degree

**Deferred 2026-10-12.** Not rejected. Not scheduled.

## What was proposed

Letting a site list two or more degrees, such as a bachelor's and a
master's, in Education and on the timeline.

## Why it was not built

The engine came from a site with one degree. Its timeline draws the degree
as one band behind every lane, places each other entry as before, in or
after that band, and keeps the band's label in view as the page scrolls.
Two bands raise questions the copy does not answer: overlapping bands, two
labels, and which band an entry is "in". The build refuses a second degree
with a message naming the limit, rather than drawing it wrongly.

## What could and could not be compared

Nothing was measured: no user exists yet. That many readers of a
portfolio have more than one degree is likely but is not evidenced here.

## The trigger

A user, or a contributor's design, needs two degrees; the first issue
asking for it is the trigger.

## What revisiting would have to produce

A drawing of two bands on the wide timeline and in the phone list, checked
by the standing checks; Education listing every degree; and the content
schema's band lane allowing more than one, with the timeline's placement
rules restated.
